Concrete coatings for Havasu homes, toys and businesses

Life in Lake Havasu City happens outside, and a lot of it starts in the garage. That is where the boat waits on its trailer, the side-by-side gets hosed off, the coolers get loaded and the wet towels land after a day on the water. It is also where the concrete takes the most punishment.

We coat that concrete so it lasts. Our crews grind and repair the slab, then install a system that suits how you use the space, whether that is a garage floor coating with a sand-hiding flake finish, a cooler, slip-resistant pool deck, a seamless interior floor for a lake house, or a tough commercial floor for a marina shop or restaurant.

Not sure where to start? Most people begin with the garage, then come back for the pool deck or an interior room once they see the difference. Others coat everything at once to get it done before summer. Either way, we will walk the property with you and price each area separately.

Every quote is free, done on site and written down, so you know exactly what you are getting before anything starts.

Garage, RV and boat bay coatings

A Havasu garage is rarely just a garage. It is a boat shed, an RV bay, a gym, a beer fridge and a workshop, and its floor sees hot tires in July, lake water off the trailer and sand tracked in from the beach. Bare concrete stains, dusts and cracks under all of that. Cheap coatings peel.

What heat, sun and sand do to Havasu concrete

Lake Havasu City is one of the hottest places in the country, and it shows on concrete. Garage slabs bake all afternoon, tires come home hot enough to soften cheap coatings, and the low western sun pours into open garages and across pool decks. Add fine wind-blown sand, hard water spots from sprinklers and lake water, and occasional heavy summer storms, and you have a tough environment for any floor.

Our flooring installation process

Most two- and three-car garages take one to two days. RV bays, pool decks and commercial spaces get a written schedule up front.

  1. On-site quote

    We measure, check the slab for moisture, cracks and old coatings, and show you samples.

  2. Grind and repair

    Diamond grinding with dust control, then crack and pit repair for a sound surface.

  3. Base coat

    A bonding base coat goes down while the concrete is open and clean.

  4. Color or flake

    Flake broadcast, metallic work or solid color, depending on your system.

  5. Topcoat and walkthrough

    A UV-stable, textured topcoat, then a walkthrough and care tips.

Planning your project around the Havasu calendar

There is no bad time to coat a floor here, but each season has its quirks. Late fall through early spring is the easiest stretch: slabs are cooler, materials behave and it is the quietest time for most businesses and rental owners. It is also when we have the most flexibility on dates.

Spring fills up fast as people get ready for boating season, so book early if you want the garage finished before the boat comes out of storage.

Summer installs are routine for us, but they run on an early schedule. Crews arrive at first light, materials stay in the shade and batches are sized to the slab temperature. Fast-curing systems mean the garage is usually back in use quickly.

Outdoor projects in late summer are planned around storm forecasts, since decks and patios need dry weather while coatings set. We will suggest the best window for your project when we quote it.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what Havasu homeowners ask most. Need more? Call (928) 555-0164.

Can you coat an RV or boat garage?

Yes, and they are some of our favorite projects. Large bays need a system that handles heavy tires, trailer jacks and the water that drips off a boat after a day on the lake. We usually recommend a flexible base coat, a flake broadcast and a textured topcoat, priced by the actual square footage of the bay.

How does a coating hold up to Havasu summer heat?

Heat is the main reason garage floors fail here. Hot tires soften weak coatings and pull them off the slab. We diamond-grind every floor so the coating bonds into the concrete, use base coats and topcoats rated for high temperatures, and install early in the day when slabs are coolest.

Will a pool deck coating be too hot to walk on?

Any surface in full July sun gets hot. Light colors and textured finishes stay noticeably more comfortable than dark or smooth ones, which is why we steer pool deck clients toward pale sand and soft grey tones with a knockdown or broadcast texture.

Can you finish between vacation rental bookings?

Often, yes. Fast-curing polyaspartic systems let many garages and patios go from bare concrete to usable within a day or two. Tell us your booking calendar and we will plan around it.

What about the sand we track in from the beach?

Sand is abrasive and will slowly dull any finish, but a flake floor hides it well and a quality topcoat resists wear. Sweeping or blowing out the garage regularly, plus a mat inside the door, keeps it looking good for years.

Do you work on the Island and in Desert Hills?

Yes. We cover all of Lake Havasu City including the Island, plus Desert Hills, Horizon Six, Crystal Beach, Golden Shores and down toward Parker. See the service areas section for more.

Do you coat garages for homes that are listed for sale?

Yes. A finished garage photographs well and is a quick, relatively affordable upgrade before listing. Fast-cure systems keep the garage out of use for only a short time.

How much does a garage floor coating cost?

It depends on size, slab condition, repairs and the system you choose. A standard two-car garage and a 50-foot RV bay are very different jobs. We measure in person and give you a written quote with clear options.
