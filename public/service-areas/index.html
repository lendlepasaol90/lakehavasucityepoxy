Areas we serve along the river

Based in Lake Havasu City and working up and down this stretch of the Colorado River.

Coating work in this climate depends on timing. In summer, we often start at first light so the slab and materials stay cool enough to work properly. Staying close to Lake Havasu City lets us do that reliably and keep projects moving.

These are the communities we cover most. If you are outside them, call us anyway; we may still be able to help, especially with larger projects.

Lake Havasu City

All of town, north to south. Garages, RV bays, pool decks, patios and interiors, plus commercial and industrial projects.

The Island

Homes, rentals and businesses across the London Bridge on Pittsburgh Point, where sand and lake water come with the territory.

Havasu Foothills Estates

Larger homes with oversized garages and RV bays, often with view decks and pools that benefit from cool, textured coatings.

Desert Hills

North of town, with bigger lots, shops and outbuildings. A good fit for flexible, heavy-duty garage and shop systems.

Horizon Six

Homes and workshops along Highway 95 with garages and storage buildings that see hard use.

Crystal Beach

Lakeside homes and second homes north of the city, many with boat garages and patios.

Golden Shores

Homes and storage near Topock and the river, where heat and dust are part of daily life.

Parker

Commercial and residential projects along the Parker Strip and in town, by arrangement.

Needles, CA

Selected residential and commercial projects just across the Colorado River.
